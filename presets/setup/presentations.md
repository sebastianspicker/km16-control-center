# Presentations: PowerPoint for Mac

This preset targets Microsoft PowerPoint (`com.microsoft.Powerpoint`) and combines slide preparation and delivery. It cannot detect whether PowerPoint is editing or presenting.

The top two rows control delivery: start from the beginning or current slide, Presenter View, exit, black or white screen, laser, and arrow pointer. The bottom rows cover editing: new or duplicate slide, Undo, Redo, Save, comment, hyperlink, and background formatting.

The upper-left dial moves through slides or animation steps and presses to advance. The upper-right moves focus through objects, links, or presenter controls and presses to activate the selection. The main dial controls editing zoom and presses to fit the slide. It does not magnify a slide show.

Open the presentation in PowerPoint, enable live actions, and grant Accessibility permission. Use slide-show controls only while presenting: B and W otherwise type characters, Command-A selects all while editing, and Return or Space may insert text. Tab also behaves differently while editing text. PowerPoint keeps control of focus and selection; KM16 Control Center does not click the slide canvas or guess the current mode. Enable macOS Keyboard navigation if presenter controls cannot receive focus.

Check the mappings against any customized Office shortcuts. The preset uses keyboard actions only and needs no Office add-in, AppleScript, or macro.
